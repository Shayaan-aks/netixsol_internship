Collingwood has claimed its 16th AFL premiership with a thrilling four-point victory over the Brisbane Lions in the 2023 Grand Final at the MCG.

Bobby Hill was the hero for Collingwood, kicking four goals to win the Norm Smith Medal as best on ground.
