# Enterprise Roadmap

This roadmap defines the evolution of the NetixSol AI Platform over the next 12-18 months.

## Phase 1: Omnichannel Expansion (Q1)
- **WhatsApp Integration:** Deploy the agent to WhatsApp Business API via Twilio. Allow users to text/voice note properties.
- **Web Widget:** Embed a chat widget on the NetixSol website.
- **Multilingual Support:** Auto-detect and switch between Urdu, English, Punjabi, and Pashto.

## Phase 2: CRM & Sales Automation (Q2)
- **Enterprise CRM Connectors:** Deep integration with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho.
- **Lead Nurturing:** Automated follow-up calls or WhatsApp messages for leads that didn't book an appointment.
- **Predictive Lead Scoring:** Use machine learning to score leads based on conversation sentiment, budget, and historical conversion data.

## Phase 3: Advanced AI Capabilities (Q3)
- **Brand Voice Personalities:** Allow different agent personas (e.g., highly professional vs. casual and friendly) based on the client demographic.
- **Emotion Adaptation:** If the customer's voice indicates frustration, immediately soften the AI's tone and prioritize human escalation.
- **Agent Self-Evaluation:** After every call, a background LLM process grades the agent's performance and adjusts prompt weights automatically.

## Phase 4: Full Ecosystem Integration (Q4)
- **Live MLS & Inventory Feeds:** Direct API connections to government land registries or developer inventory systems.
- **Mortgage Calculators:** AI can dynamically calculate and propose mortgage payment plans during the conversation.
- **Digital Signatures:** Initiate document signing workflows directly from the voice call.
